What we do

Turn real moments into stories people remember.

Media Club brings students together behind the camera and inside the control room. We learn by doing: planning coverage, making creative decisions, and delivering work for real school events.

●

Produce live

Build livestreams and recordings as camera operators, directors, mixers, OBS operators and supervisors working as one team.

◆

Capture the moment

Practise framing, light, timing, sound and movement so an ordinary photo or video becomes something worth keeping.

▲

Shape the story

Direct, edit, mix sound, create graphics and interview people. The finished story depends on every crew decision.

Inside Media Club

There is a role for every kind of storyteller.

Start with one role, see how the whole crew works, and take on more responsibility as your confidence grows. Beginners are welcome.
